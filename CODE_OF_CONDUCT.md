# Code of Conduct

Be respectful.

Do not be an idiot:

- No harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- No hate speech or intentionally offensive behavior.
- Do not share anyone's private information.
- Criticize ideas, not people.
- Keep discussions constructive and welcoming.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary.

If someone violates these rules, report it privately to the project maintainer.
